## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Per-deck Learn & Review settings, accessible from Anki's existing deck options menu.
- Support for alternative card presentations with customizable front, back, and additional CSS.
- Ability to initialize an alternative presentation from an existing Anki card template.
- Independent selection of the original or alternative presentation for Learn and Review sessions.
- Alternative card rendering without creating duplicate cards or changing their scheduling.
